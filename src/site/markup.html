How it works

Three steps. Then help can start.

  1. 01

    Carry

    Wallet card or a sticker on a phone, helmet, or bag. It stays with you.

  2. 02

    Tap to scan

    A responder taps with any NFC phone. No app, no mobile data, no Wi-Fi.

  3. 03

    Help starts

    Name, blood type, emergency contact, and allergies appear as plain text.

The problem

First responders should not have to guess.

In an emergency the person who needs care often cannot speak. Allergies, blood type, and who to call are the details that change the first minutes — and they are useless if they live only in a phone that is locked.

The emergency details are stored as plain text on the tag. A compatible phone reads what is already there.
